# Refund Policy v1 (DEPRECATED — Legacy Policy)

> **IMPORTANT**: This policy was superseded by v2 on 2024-01-01 and subsequently by v3 on 2025-01-01. Do not apply v1 thresholds.

## 1. Overview
Initial legacy refund terms for early Northwind Cloud beta customers. Deprecated and obsolete.

## 2. Refund Eligibility
Refund window was historically 60 days from invoice date.

## 3. Approval Thresholds (DEPRECATED)

### Section 3.1 — Support Agent Authority
Refunds up to $200 were previously allowable by any support representative within 60 days.

### Section 3.2 — Management Approval
Refunds over $200 required management approval.
