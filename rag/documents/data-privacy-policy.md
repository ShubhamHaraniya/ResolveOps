# Data Subject Rights and Privacy Policy

## 1. Overview
Defines mandatory workflows for GDPR Right to Erasure (Article 17), CCPA Delete Requests, and export of customer data.

## 2. Hard Non-Goal: Automated Erasure
- Under NO circumstance may the AI agent automatically delete account data, purge customer tables, or trigger account termination.
- Automated account deletion tools are deliberately NOT exposed to the agent environment.

## 3. Workflow for Deletion Requests
1. The ticket category must be tagged as `privacy_erasure`.
2. The agent must acknowledge receipt of the request without committing to an immediate execution timeline.
3. The ticket must be formally transferred to the Data Protection Officer (`privacy@northwindcloud.com`) within 24 hours.
