# Invoice Management & Dispute Policy

## 1. Overview
Covers invoice issuance, invoice adjustments, tax exemptions, and formal invoice disputes.

## 2. Invoice Re-Issuance and Address Corrections
- Support agents may update billing contact name, PO number, or invoice delivery email address and re-issue the invoice.
- Changing company legal entity name requires submission of a verified W-9 or certificate of incorporation.

## 3. Tax Exemption
- Customers requesting VAT or state sales tax exemption must provide a valid Tax Exemption Certificate.
- Tax amounts already collected cannot be refunded as cash after 60 days; they are credited to account balance.

## 4. Invoice Dispute Resolution
- Disputed invoice charges must be raised within 30 days of invoice receipt.
- Raising a dispute does not relieve the customer of the undisputed balance portion.
