# User Seat Allocation & Licensing Policy

## 1. Overview
Governs seat additions, minimum commitment quotas, and inactive seat reassignment across Northwind Cloud organizations.

## 2. Minimum and Maximum Limits
- Starter tier: Minimum 1 seat, hard maximum 5 seats. Additional users require upgrading to Pro.
- Pro tier: Minimum 5 seats, maximum 50 seats. Organizations needing more than 50 seats must migrate to Enterprise.
- Enterprise tier: Minimum 20 seats, no upper bound.

## 3. License Reassignment
- Account administrators may revoke inactive member licenses and reassign them to new team members at zero charge.
- Deleting a user does NOT automatically reduce billed seat count unless explicit seat downscale is requested.
