# Service Credits and SLA Compensation Policy

## 1. Overview
Service credits are non-cash monetary credits applied to a Northwind Cloud customer account balance to offset future invoice payments.

## 2. SLA Breach Service Credits
Service Level Agreement compensation is awarded based on documented production platform downtime:
- 99.0% to 99.9% uptime: 10% monthly service credit.
- 95.0% to 98.9% uptime: 25% monthly service credit.
- Below 95.0% uptime: 50% monthly service credit.

## 3. Discretionary Goodwill Credits
- Support agents may issue goodwill credits up to $100 per incident for verified platform degradation.
- Support leads must approve credits between $100 and $500.
- Credits exceeding $500 require Finance Controller authorization.
- Maximum cumulative credit per calendar year cannot exceed one month of account contract value.
