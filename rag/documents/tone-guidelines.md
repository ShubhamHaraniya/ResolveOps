# Customer Communication & Tone Guidelines

## 1. Overview
Guidelines for drafting customer replies, internal ticket notes, and operational status explanations across all Northwind Cloud customer support channels.

## 2. Voice & Tone Principles
- **Empathetic & Attentive**: Acknowledge customer frustration directly without using defensive language or excuses.
- **Accurate & Grounded**: State only verified account facts. Never guess dates, invoice totals, or technical root causes.
- **Concise & Actionable**: Provide clear next steps and timeline expectations. Avoid corporate jargon.
- **No Unauthorized Commitments**: Do NOT promise refunds, discounts, SLA credits, or product roadmap delivery dates unless approved by policy.

## 3. Human Review Requirement
All outbound customer responses drafted by AI agents must undergo Human Review and explicit approval before transmission.
