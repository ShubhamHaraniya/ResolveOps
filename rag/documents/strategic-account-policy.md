# Strategic & High-ARR Account Engagement Policy

## 1. Overview
Defines specialized protections for VIP, enterprise, and high-ARR accounts (`strategic: true` or Annual Recurring Revenue > $50,000).

## 2. Prohibition on Autonomous Writes
- Automated writes (including self-serve refunds, auto-tier changes, or automated cancellations) must NEVER execute automatically on strategic accounts.
- Any proposed action requires explicit Human Approval by the Customer Success Lead or Account Executive.

## 3. Communication Safeguards
- Outbound responses to strategic accounts must never use generic auto-replies.
- All draft responses require approval by the dedicated Account Executive before dispatch.
- Fast turnaround SLA (< 1 hour) applies during business hours.
