# Subscription Plans & Entitlements Policy

## 1. Overview
Details feature tiers, minimum commitments, user limits, and plan change policies for Northwind Cloud offerings.

## 2. Product Tiers
- **Starter Plan**: $29/seat/month. Designed for small teams. Max 5 seats. Community support.
- **Pro Plan**: $79/seat/month. Advanced analytics, 99.9% SLA, SSO integration. Max 50 seats. Standard support SLA (4 hours).
- **Enterprise Plan**: Custom pricing (minimum $20,000 ARR). Dedicated VPC deployment, unlimited seats, custom IAM, 24/7 phone support with 15-minute critical SLA.

## 3. Plan Changes
- Self-serve upgrades from Starter to Pro are supported mid-cycle with instant proration.
- Upgrades to Enterprise require contracting through Sales.
- Plan cancellations require 30 days advance notice before renewal date.
